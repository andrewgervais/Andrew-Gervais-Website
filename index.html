Ohio Tax Capitalization Study

A triple difference-in-differences study of whether Ohio's 2014 Homestead Exemption expansion was capitalized into home prices. I built a panel of 150+ Ohio ZIP codes from Zillow ZHVI and Census ACS data, then estimated effects with clustered standard errors, event-study plots, and placebo tests. Price effects were largest in high-senior-share ZIP codes in slower-growing metros and attenuated in Columbus, consistent with housing supply elasticity theory. Results held up under alternative treatment cutoffs and pre-trend tests.

Python Econometrics Triple DiD Event Study Census ACS API

Patient Flow Analysis — Stormont Vail Health

Built for DataFest. I joined patient, diagnosis, provider, department, and social-determinants tables covering 7.6M encounters at a Kansas health system. I found that 5% of patients drove 35% of total volume, and 72% of them juggled 2–4 uncoordinated specialty schedules. I validated this against county-level population health data. I then proposed a coordinated-scheduling redesign estimated to recover ~98,000 appointment slots ($19.6M), cut transit costs by ~75%, and eliminate 300K+ redundant touchpoints a year.

Python Pandas Data Visualization Healthcare Analytics

Robot Rental Management Database

Final project for my databases course. I designed a 15-table relational database for a fictional robot-rental company and normalized every table to BCNF, decomposing the robots and driverless-cars tables to remove transitive dependencies. I added 9 indexes on the most-joined columns, triggers that prevent a robot from being double-booked, and views that summarize rentals, ratings, and revenue by robot model. On top of the database I built a Java command-line app over JDBC that handles customer and robot management, rentals, returns, deliveries, and reports. It uses prepared statements to block SQL injection and transactions that roll back on failure.

SQL SQLite Java JDBC Database Design
